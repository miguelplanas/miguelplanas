# Miguel Planas

I'm a Data & AI Engineer at Inditex, and I'm studying for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at UDC, in A Coruña.

What I enjoy most is taking a model that works in a notebook and turning it into something people actually use.

## Some of my work

- [MXNJ](https://miguelplanas.dev/projects/mxnj/): find every garment in a fashion photo among 27,000 products. Two prizes at HackUDC 2026.
- [MXP AutoScan](https://miguelplanas.dev/projects/mxp-autoscan/): photos of a damaged car in, an appraisal and a repair estimate out. First prize at the DIHGIGAL Datathon.
- [LocalFold](https://miguelplanas.dev/projects/localfold/): fold a protein on a supercomputer without ever opening a terminal. Second at Impacthon.
- [Docs into videos](https://miguelplanas.dev/projects/final-year-project/): my final year project, which turns technical documentation into narrated videos in several languages.
- [OpenTabs](https://github.com/miguelplanas/open-tabs): my own guitar tab library, made for reading on the phone while I play.

The rest is on [miguelplanas.dev](https://miguelplanas.dev).
